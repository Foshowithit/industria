What This Is

A thin slice of INDUSTRIA — a playable model of the physical economy — exposed so you can poke it. It is one of four intended sub-engines.

The thesis

The product is not photorealism. It is legibility: every visible consequence traceable to a cause the player was never shown directly. A cut fails because the tool was long, not because a dice roll said so — and the game can tell you which in numbers a machinist recognises.

kc(h) = kc₁.₁ · h^−mc Fc = kc · b · h MRR = ae · ap · f Pc = kc · MRR / 6.0e7 kW F̄ = 60000 · Pc / vc δtip = F · L³ / (8·E·I) , I = π·d⁴/64 ap_crit = 1 / (2 · kc · Re[G]min)

What is honest here

  • Truth ≠ observation. This kernel computes engine truth. A real game would put resolution, calibration and thermal uncertainty on top before you ever see a number.
  • Constants are debt. kc₁.₁, mc, and machine dynamics (K, ζ, fn) are representative literature values, not measured per machine. The structure is the product; the constants are the work.
  • Deterministic. No clock, no unseeded randomness, no hidden state. Same inputs, same output, forever — which is what makes replay and reference-case CI possible.
  • No production authority. Nothing here is posted CAM or a safe program. It is arithmetic with an audit trail.
